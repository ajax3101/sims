# Product overview

SIMS is for the staff who maintain student records at a Ukrainian higher education institution. The main workspace is the student register. From there, staff can find a student, review their current status, and open the full record.

## Records

A student record connects a person to their study group, specialty, educational programme, level, and form of study. It also holds the student's group and status history, grades, academic debts, contracts, payments, and documents.

Groups are identified by their institutional ciphers. The cipher contains cohort information used to calculate a group's current course for an academic year. Specialty records support both the 2015 numeric classifier and the 2024 letter classifier, with links between successor specialties where available.

## Daily work

Staff can search and filter the register, maintain reference data, import DOCX or XLSX rosters, review student cards, and export reports. The reports cover academic debts, tuition debts, and an individual student's record.

The interface uses Ukrainian higher education terms, including «освітня програма», «форма здобуття освіти», «залікова книжка», «академвідпустка» and «перескладання».

## Data handling

The source rosters are private working files and are excluded from Git. The repository does not include real grades, debt records, or contracts. Any synthetic records must be marked as demo data in the interface.

Authentication and user roles are not implemented yet. The app is intended for local development until access control is added.
